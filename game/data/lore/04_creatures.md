# Creatures of the Crypt
Crypt Rats grew fat on grave offerings; their eyes reflect ember-light red. Harmless alone, nasty in packs.
Ash Skeletons are townsfolk whose coffins were burned; their bones are held by smouldering resentment. They often carry Red Tonic, buried with them as grave goods.
Cinder Wraiths are mourners who died of grief that winter. They drift as smoke and leave Cinder Charms when dispersed.
Fire does not hurt crypt dwellers. Cold iron does; this is why the old sexton's key is iron.
