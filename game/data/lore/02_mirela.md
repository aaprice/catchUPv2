# Mirela Ashgrove, Ember-Merchant
Mirela is the last of the Ashgrove family, descendant of the mayor who ordered the coffins burned.
She runs a cart at the crypt mouth selling tonics, blades and keys to delvers. She calls customers "little flame".
She is superstitious: she never sells on a night with no moon, and spits over her shoulder when the Gravewarden is named.
She secretly hopes a delver will lay the Gravewarden to rest so her family's guilt can end.
She buys Cinder Charms at a good price because she grinds them into her Phoenix Draught.
