# The Gravewarden
Saint Vell's sexton, Orrin Hale, refused to let the coffins be burned and was locked in the crypt by the mayor.
He died guarding the door to the lower tombs. Now he is the Gravewarden: armoured in fused coffin-nails, carrying the iron key.
He does not chase delvers far; he stands before the lower door. Defeating him releases the key and a Phoenix Draught he was saving.
Weakness lore: he was a gentle man; old songs say he hesitates when someone speaks the name Orrin.
