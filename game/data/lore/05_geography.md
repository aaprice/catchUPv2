# Geography of the Crypt
Upper Crypt: winding ossuary corridors starting at the chapel stairs. Rats and skeletons.
The Warden's Hall: a long gallery ending at a single iron-bound door to the Lower Tombs.
Lower Tombs: one wide corridor leading to the Ember Well, the exit to the surface ruins beyond.
The air grows warmer with depth. Delvers report hearing a man humming a hymn near the iron door.
