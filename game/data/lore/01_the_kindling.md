# The Kindling
Three centuries ago the town of Ashgrove buried its dead in limestone catacombs beneath the chapel of Saint Vell.
During the Long Winter the town burned its own coffins for warmth. The dead did not forgive this.
Embers from that winter never fully went out; they sank into the crypt and now smoulder in bone and stone.
Locals call the place the Ember Crypt. Children dare each other to touch its iron gate, which is always warm.
